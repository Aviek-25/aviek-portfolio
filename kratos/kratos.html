PROJECT OVERVIEW

More than a voice.

02 / OVERVIEW

KRATOS AI is a personal voice-assistant project built around the idea of making everyday computer interaction more natural. The project explores the use of Python, voice interaction and AI model APIs to create an assistant that can understand requests and support useful workflows. Its long-term vision is to bring multiple AI capabilities together in one assistant.
