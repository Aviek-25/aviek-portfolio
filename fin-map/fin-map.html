THE PROJECT

Money, mapped clearly.

Fin MAP is a personal finance tracker designed to make managing money simpler. It provides a centralized place to record income and expenses, create monthly budgets, review transactions, and understand spending patterns.

View income, expenses, and your overall financial position.

Financial Analytics

Explore spending categories and understand cash flow.
